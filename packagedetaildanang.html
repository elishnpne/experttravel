DANANG [5 Nights/6 Days]

"Explore Da Nang, Central Vietnam’s vibrant coastal city, famous for its stunning beaches, modern attractions, scenic mountains, and iconic sites like Dragon Bridge, Marble Mountains, and Ba Na Hills’ Golden Bridge."

Day1: Danang Arrival → Checkin and Overnight at the hotel.

Upon arrival at Danang Airport, complete immigration formalities and meet our driver for transfer to the hotel.Check-in after 2:00PM and enjoy the rest of the day at leisure to relax or explore the city on your own.Overnight stayin Danang.

Day 2: Danang → Ba Na Hills → Golden Bridge → Danang

After breakfast, depart for a full-day tour to Ba Na Hills. Enjoy a scenic cable car ride offering panoramic mountain views and visit Le Jardin D’Amour Flower Garden, The highlight of the day is the iconic Golden Bridge, held by two giant stone hands overlooking the misty mountains. Continue to Linh Ung Pagoda and Linh Chua Linh Tu Temple for peaceful surroundings. Later, visit the French Square and spend some fun time at Fantasy Park. Return to Danang in the evening and overnight at the hotel.

Day 3: Danang → Vin Wonder → Hoi An Ancient Town

After breakfast, transfer to Vin Wonder Nam Hoi An, an exciting theme and cultural park combining entertainment and heritage. Explore ethnic minority architecture such as E De, Co Tu, and Tay houses, and visit the Craft Village Street to meet artisans and witness traditional crafts.. In the afternoon, visit Hoi An Ancient Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site once a bustling trading port. Discover its charming streets, traditional merchant houses, the 400-year-old Japanese Covered Bridge, and the vibrant local market. Return to Danang for an overnight stay.

Day 4: Danang City Tour & Shopping

Enjoy breakfast at the hotel, followed by a relaxed morning at the beach. Later, explore Danang City with visits to Love Bridge and the city’s famous Dragon, Han, and Tran Thi Ly Bridges. Continue to Son Tra Peninsula to see Linh Ung Bai But Pagoda with the tallest Lady Buddha statue in Vietnam, offering breathtaking sea views and chances to spot monkeys. Visit Han Market to shop for local specialties and souvenirs before enjoying free time in the evening to explore the city’s nightlife.

Day 5: Danang Free Day

After breakfast, spend the entire day at your own pace. Relax on the beach, shop for local goods, or enjoy optional activities in and around the city. Overnight stay in Danang.

Day 6: Danang Departure

After breakfast, check out from the hotel. You will be transferred to Danang Airport for your flight back home with wonderful memories of your Vietnam trip.After arrival at Danang airport, immigration procedure. Welcomed by our driver and transfer to hotel.
