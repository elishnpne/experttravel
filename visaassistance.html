Tourist Visa – Nepal Important Guidelines

Tourist Visa is issued according to the immigration laws of Nepal. Applicants must strictly follow the rules set by the Department of Immigration.

Key Requirements:

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Recent passport size photograph
  • Completed visa application form
  • Confirmed return air ticket
  • Proof of accommodation (hotel booking / invitation)
  • Sufficient financial proof for stay

Important Rules:

  • Tourist visa cannot be used for employment
  • Overstay results in fine & legal action
  • Visa extension must be done before expiry
  • Maximum stay period is subject to immigration law
